Web Continuator Guide

A compact overview of the app, the musical flow, and how sessions are saved.

What It Does

Web Continuator captures a phrase from browser MIDI, sends it to the Python Continuator engine, and plays back the continuation either through the built-in browser synth or a MIDI output port.

Quick Start

  1. Create or open a session.
  2. Click Connect MIDI and choose an input.
  3. Play a short phrase on your keyboard.
  4. Wait for the phrase to end, or click Send Phrase.
  5. Review the continuation in the piano rolls, history, and memory.

Studio Layout

  • Perform: live MIDI capture, phrase generation, replay, and infinite mode.
  • Sound: playback routing plus built-in and custom Faust renderers.
  • Memory: corpus import, active engine memory, and phrase history.
  • Session: session lifecycle, advanced Continuator settings, and saved sessions.

History vs Memory

History is the logged record of phrases sent and generated for a session. Memory is the live state currently active inside the Continuator engine. They are related, but not always identical.

Accounts and Saved Sessions

Guest mode works immediately. Sign in only if you want sessions saved under your username so you can reopen them later. The account menu lives in the top-right header and includes sign in, sign out, and saved-session access.

History and References

Continuator was developed by François Pachet at Sony CSL Paris in 1999 as an interactive musical system that learns a performer’s style in real time and answers with stylistically coherent continuations. The foundational paper introduced the idea of a playable “virtual musician”, later articles described the instrument in practice and in educational settings, and the current Python implementation extends the approach with more explicit controllable constraints.

  • GitHub: fpachet/Continuator
  • The Continuator: Musical Interaction With Style (2003)
  • Beyond the Cybernetic Jam Fantasy: the Continuator (2004)
  • Young Children Confronting the Continuator (2006)
  • François Pachet publications and AI music notes

Performance Notes

  • Auto-send phrase starts generation after the phrase ends.
  • A phrase closes after the selected phrase gap following the final note-off.
  • Infinite mode keeps chaining continuations until you stop it.
  • Playing live MIDI interrupts whatever continuation is currently playing.
